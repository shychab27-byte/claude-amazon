Supports any of these Seller Central reports: FBA Manage Inventory, Inventory Health, Restocking, Inventory Ledger, All Orders. Drop multiple files at once. You can also include a Season Map CSV to specify each SKU's season.

🗺️ Season Map CSV (optional but recommended)

Create a simple CSV with two columns — sku and season — to tell Claude exactly which season each product belongs to. Without it, Claude will infer from product names. Drop it in along with your other reports.

Example file: season-map.csv
